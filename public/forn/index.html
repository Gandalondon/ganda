You pass history every day.

Most of it, you never notice.

Forn notices it for you.

It quietly notes the old places you pass while you get on with your day. Your location never leaves your phone.

At 9pm, find out what you passed.

Up to five finds, the most interesting first.

Keep it, or let it go.

Swipe right if it’s interesting. Swipe left and it turns to dust.

Every day adds a ring.

Over time it becomes a picture of everything you’ve learned, just by living your life.

Coming soon to Android.

Privacy

Forn keeps everything on your phone: the places you passed, when, and your answers. No account, no ads, and no track of where you went.

Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose Tell me more, which shares that one place with the app you pick.

This page uses no cookies and no tracking.
